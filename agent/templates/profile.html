What each restriction type does
movement pattern
Blocks a pattern everywhere, e.g. Vertical Push for a cranky shoulder. Usually the best choice for a joint problem.
exercise
Blocks one specific exercise by its database id.
equipment
Blocks everything requiring that implement.
posture
e.g. Hanging if grip or shoulder hanging is a problem.
body region
Very broad — Lower Body removes most of a program.
plane of motion
e.g. Transverse Plane to avoid loaded rotation after a back injury.
classification
e.g. Plyometric when impact is contraindicated.
max difficulty
A ceiling from 1 (Beginner) to 8 (Legendary). Value is the number.
